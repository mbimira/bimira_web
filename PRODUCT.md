# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Plain static HTML, CSS, and a little JavaScript; no framework. Deploy to GitHub Pages or Cloudflare Pages, with a custom domain later if desired. Dashboards and demos are embedded or linked from where they are hosted.

## Users

Primary: hiring managers, recruiters, and potential clients visiting the portfolio from a resume, LinkedIn, or an email link, usually on a laptop or phone, deciding within a minute or two whether the site owner is worth a conversation. Their job: quickly see what he does (data analytics and applied AI), check that claims are backed by real projects and results, get a sense of how he thinks and communicates, and find an easy way to contact him.
Secondary: the site owner, who updates the site as he finishes new projects.

## Product Purpose

A portfolio that lets a visitor verify skills in a couple of minutes instead of taking claims on faith. Each project is a short case study — the problem, the data, the method, the result — with live dashboards or demos where possible. It shows how the owner thinks through real analysis and applied AI work, not just a list of tools; the contact path is always one click away.

## Positioning

A portfolio built on demonstrated work rather than listed claims: visitors inspect real analysis and applied AI case studies instead of a resume's tool list, with proof front and center and contact one click away.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from a resume, LinkedIn profile, or email link on a laptop or phone and decide within a minute or two. Projects and demos live where they are hosted and are embedded or linked from the site. The owner adds a case study each time a project finishes; the site is otherwise mostly read-only.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Short case studies per project (problem, data, method, result); live dashboards or demos where possible; contact one click away from every page; static HTML/CSS/JavaScript with no framework; free static hosting; dashboards and demos embedded or externally hosted. No user accounts, CMS, or dynamic backend.

## Evidence on Hand

No projects, dashboards, demo links, or case-study content exist yet — future work must not fabricate testimonials, metrics, customers, or benchmarks. The owner's background (data analytics, applied AI, SQL, Python, Tableau, Power BI) is source material to draw from, not content to publish as-is.

## Product Principles

1. Show the work: every capability claim is backed by a case study with problem, data, method, and result — never a tool list alone.
2. Earn the decision in two minutes: scannable hierarchy, the strongest proof visible without scrolling, contact one click away.
3. Works on phone and laptop: visitors arrive from both; the experience must hold on each.
4. Near-zero upkeep: static and framework-free, so adding a finished project means adding one case-study page.
5. Real work only: no fabricated testimonials, metrics, customers, or benchmarks.
